Install & first login

Five steps from invitation to your alumni home.

1

Download the APK

Use the button or QR above. The file is about ~190 MB.

2

Allow installs

Android will ask permission for your browser to install apps — tap Allow. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.

3

Install & open

Tap the downloaded file, then Install. Find the Alumni Connect icon and open it.

4

Join the demo network

During local testing, connect to the same WiFi as the Reunio server. A deployed release will use a public server instead.

5

Use your invitation

Sign in with your email or alumni number and the temporary password sent by the Alumni Office. Change it when prompted; if it expired, use Forgot Password.

Common questions

Who can sign in?

The Alumni Office creates and invites accounts. You can sign in with the email address or alumni number on your record. Graduate-only career features unlock once your school year is confirmed as Graduate.

How does the invitation work?

Your invitation contains your sign-in identifiers, a temporary password valid for seven days, and the official app download link. You must change that password after your first sign-in.

Is the app free?

Yes — Reunio is free for authorized alumni, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Which phones are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer. iPhones aren't supported by direct download (Apple only allows App Store installs) — ask us for a guided demo instead.

Why must I be on the same WiFi?

Only the current local test setup requires it: the app connects to a Reunio server on the same private network. A hosted production release will work over the internet instead.

Is “install unknown apps” safe?

You're installing our signed APK directly instead of via the Play Store — that's why Android asks. Only install the file from this page or the official QR, never from forwarded copies.

Is my data safe?

Reunio uses authenticated access, hashed passwords, role-based admin permissions, and a centralized database managed by the school. Directory visibility is also under the alumnus's control.

What if my year level is incorrect?

Use the in-app year-correction request. Once the Alumni Office approves Graduate status, career, profile, directory, résumé, job, and document tools become available.
